# Privacy Policy

Linkding Extension does not collect, store, or share any data with the developer or third parties.

The extension sends the pages you bookmark and their metadata only to the linkding server you configure. Your server URL and API token are stored locally in Safari's extension storage on your device.

The app includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/brettinternet/linkding-extension/issues.
